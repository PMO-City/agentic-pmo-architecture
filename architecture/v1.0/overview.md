# Agentic PMO: public overview

## The short definition

The Agentic PMO is a governed operating model for turning organizational intent into coordinated execution through continuous collaboration between humans and AI.

It is not simply a project-management application with an AI assistant. It treats governance, evidence, authority, explainability, current state, and organizational learning as part of operation itself.

PMO City is the steward and first implementation associated with this reference architecture. This public edition describes the operating model; it does not claim that every PMO City feature or every other product already conforms to it.

## The problem

Organizations operate continuously, but management systems often operate periodically.

Important information is reconstructed from meetings, documents, tickets, spreadsheets, messages, and personal memory. Governance is frequently checked after execution. Different teams see different slices of reality. AI tools can produce useful outputs, but their authority, evidence, and accountability are often unclear.

The result is administrative friction:

- leaders spend time rebuilding context before making decisions;
- teams coordinate through repeated status collection;
- evidence is separated from the work it describes;
- changes in the real world arrive late in management views;
- automation can act faster than governance can respond; and
- lessons are lost or turned into generic memory without validation.

The Agentic PMO addresses this by maintaining a governed relationship between intent, work, execution, evidence, current state, human judgment, and learning.

## The central idea

The architecture maintains one governed operational reality while allowing many participants and systems to see purpose-specific projections of it.

The model therefore separates:

- the organizational intent that gives work legitimacy;
- the Activity or Project through which work is governed;
- the execution performed by people, AI, systems, partners, or real-world actors;
- the Evidence produced by execution;
- the accepted current state of the organization;
- the projection prepared for a particular participant or decision; and
- the learning that is validated before it becomes durable knowledge.

These distinctions keep a dashboard from becoming the truth, an AI output from becoming authority, or a completed task from being mistaken for achieved value.

## The nine-step loop

The operating model is described as a continuous nine-step loop:

1. Organizational intent gives work a legitimate direction.
2. Governed Activities and Projects make that direction accountable and bounded.
3. Humans and governed AI execute within explicit authority and technical boundaries.
4. Execution creates Evidence and changes operational conditions.
5. Accepted Evidence updates the Living Governed State.
6. Participants receive access-aware Workspace Projections and Decision Landscapes.
7. Humans exercise judgment and initiate governed follow-up.
8. Experience is assessed and validated as organizational learning.
9. Validated learning becomes Digital DNA that improves future operation.

The loop can begin from a new mandate, a decision, an event, an emerging need, or a change in the operating environment. It does not require every activity to be a Project. It does require the applicable authority, accountability, evidence, and state responsibilities to remain explicit.

## What makes it agentic

The architecture is agentic because AI can participate continuously in governed work rather than only answer isolated prompts. Governed AI may prepare information, coordinate activities, monitor conditions, explain state, identify attention needs, and perform bounded actions when explicitly authorized.

The architecture is governed because capability is not authority. A model that can call a tool is not automatically allowed to call it. A technically authenticated identity is not automatically accountable for a business decision. A generated summary is not automatically accepted organizational knowledge.

Humans remain responsible for judgment, leadership, accountability, trust, ethical interpretation, and the resolution of consequential ambiguity.

## What it is not

The Agentic PMO is not:

- a promise of fully autonomous management;
- a replacement for accountable human judgment;
- a generic chatbot memory layer;
- a dashboard presented as a universal source of truth;
- a new workflow engine that silently becomes business authority;
- a certification awarded merely because software interoperates; or
- a claim that one vendor or product is the only possible implementation.
