# Glossary

## Activity

The fundamental governed unit of operational work. An Activity connects purpose, ownership, accountability, authority, execution, Evidence, completion, and outcome.

## AI Assignment

A governed description of the work, scope, operations, context, Evidence, and intervention boundaries assigned to an AI participant.

## Authority

Legitimate organizational standing to direct, approve, accept, or decide within a declared scope. Technical access is not Authority.

## Benefit

A favorable effect expected or observed for an identified beneficiary or governed purpose.

## Decision Landscape

A prepared context that organizes current state, Evidence, options, uncertainty, dependencies, risk, authority, and relevant learning for human judgment.

## Digital DNA

Durable, validated organizational knowledge retained with its provenance, scope, uncertainty, and conditions of use.

## Evidence

Attributable material that supports understanding, reconciliation, acceptance, explanation, or challenge.

## Execution

The performance of governed work by a human, AI participant, system, partner, or real-world actor.

## Heartbeat

Continuous observation of governed conditions that may initiate coordination, attention, reconciliation, or escalation.

## Human judgment

Human or institutional responsibility for consequential interpretation, approval, accountability, ethics, trust, and ambiguity resolution.

## Living Governed State

The maintained representation of current organizational reality within a declared boundary, including its provenance, time, uncertainty, and acceptance status.

## Mandate

A scoped and attributable expression of legitimate authority, obligation, permission, or purpose that gives governed work its direction.

## Method Application Binding

A record of the exact governed method and version applied to an Activity or Project.

## Organizational intent

The purpose, direction, or commitment that gives governed work a reason to exist.

## Outcome

What changed as a result of execution. An Outcome does not automatically establish Benefit or Value.

## Project

A governed organizational commitment that coordinates Activities toward intended outcomes for a defined purpose.

## Retained Value

Value that remains available or useful over time under stated conditions.

## Value

An assessed significance of effects relative to purpose, stakeholders, resources, alternatives, constraints, risk, and time.

## Validated organizational learning

Learning accepted for a declared scope and purpose after evidence, context, uncertainty, and challenge have been considered.

## Workspace Projection

A purpose-specific, access-aware representation of one declared Living Governed State for an authorized participant or system.
