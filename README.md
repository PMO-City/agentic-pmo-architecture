# Agentic PMO Reference Architecture

PMO City's public reference architecture for the Agentic PMO: a governed operating model that keeps organizational intent, execution, evidence, judgment, and learning connected while humans and AI work together.

This repository is the public v1.0 reading edition. It is deliberately smaller and more practical than PMO City's internal architecture specification. It explains the model, gives a worked example, and defines the minimum ideas a reader needs before exploring implementation or conformance.

## The operating loop

Agentic PMO treats organizational operation as a continuous loop rather than a periodic reporting cycle:

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Organizational intent | A legitimate purpose, mandate, objective, or decision gives work its direction. |
| 2. Governed activities and projects | Intent is expressed as accountable, bounded work. |
| 3. Human and AI execution | People and governed AI prepare, decide, coordinate, and act within explicit boundaries. |
| 4. Evidence and operational change | Execution produces attributable evidence and changes the world being managed. |
| 5. Living Governed State | Accepted evidence updates the current representation of organizational reality. |
| 6. Workspace projections and Decision Landscapes | Participants receive purpose-specific, access-aware views for the work and decisions in front of them. |
| 7. Human judgment and governed follow-up | Humans exercise judgment, approve or redirect consequential work, and initiate follow-up. |
| 8. Validated organizational learning | Experience is assessed before it becomes reusable organizational knowledge. |
| 9. Digital DNA | Validated learning becomes durable, attributable knowledge that informs future operation. |

The loop is not a promise of autonomous management. Human judgment, authority, and accountability remain explicit throughout it.

## What this public edition contains

This edition focuses on:

- the Agentic PMO definition and problem statement;
- the nine-step operating model;
- canonical concepts and their boundaries;
- human–AI collaboration and authority;
- evidence, state, explainability, and learning;
- one worked end-to-end scenario; and
- a practical conformance boundary.

It intentionally does not publish the internal requirements corpus, private implementation details, internal deliberations, or the complete technical specification.
